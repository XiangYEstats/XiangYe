A note from me · 手书

I am always glad to connect and collaborate. 我始终乐于 交流与合作。

I remain open to future opportunities and collaborations in Bayesian computational statistics, Bayesian deep learning, probabilistic learning, latent variable modelling, causal modelling, and their applications.我愿意在贝叶斯计算统计、贝叶斯深度学习、概率学习、潜变量建模、因果建模及相关应用领域探索新的研究机会与合作可能。

I especially welcome conversations with people who enjoy exchanging ideas, developing new statistical methodology, and exploring problems together.如果你也乐于交流想法、发展新的统计方法，或一起探索有意思的问题，欢迎与我联系。
