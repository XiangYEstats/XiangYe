闲
游

Sports运动

I enjoy football, basketball, and table tennis. Swimming is my favourite sport, and I practise it weekly.平时喜欢足球、篮球和乒乓球，最喜欢游泳，也保持每周游泳的习惯。

艺

Arts绘画

I enjoy drawing. In 2011, I received First Class Prize at the National Drawing Contest for Middle School Students of China—an early chapter I remember fondly.喜欢画画。2011年曾在全国中学生绘画比赛中获得一等奖，算是一件有趣的往事。

乐

Music音乐

I enjoy music and singing. I have learned violin, piano, and saxophone, and taught myself the Chinese bamboo flute.喜欢音乐和唱歌，学过小提琴、钢琴和萨克斯，也自学过洞箫。

读

Reading阅读

I enjoy reading, with The Count of Monte Cristo and One Hundred Years of Solitude among my favourites.喜欢读书，《基督山伯爵》和《百年孤独》是其中两部最喜欢的作品。

弈

Games游戏

I enjoy competitive MOBA and FPS games, including League of Legends and Counter-Strike, especially their teamwork and strategy.喜欢玩《英雄联盟》和《反恐精英》等 MOBA 和 FPS 游戏，也喜欢和队友配合、研究战术。
